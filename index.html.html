About UTR

More than one million Rohingya refugees live in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, in the world’s largest refugee camp. The Rohingya are an ethnic minority denied citizenship in Myanmar, which has made them the world’s largest stateless population. Most arrived in 2017, fleeing persecution, large-scale violence and human rights violations.

UTR was founded on 25 August 2026 by educated Rohingya youth at Balukhali-2, Camp 12, Ukhiya. Most learning in the camps is basic education provided by UNICEF. Many young Rohingya want to study further, so they can work for their people and one day return to their own land. UTR is committed to running education in the camps.

Our programs

Four projects serving the Rohingya community inside the camps.

Education Project

Higher education for Rohingya refugees, so they can work for their people and return to Myanmar with full rights, citizenship, dignity and a safe return.

Climate Change Project

Many families live on hills where plants have been cleared. We plant trees throughout the camps for fresher air and a safer place to live.

Social Awareness Project

Emotional support and advice on health, parenting and social behavior, and campaigns to stop gender-based violence and child marriage.

Islamic Studies and Madrasa

Islamic education and a madrasa for the Muslim community in the camps, a service no other organization currently provides.
